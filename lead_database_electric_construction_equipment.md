## MARKET CONTEXT

The electric construction equipment market is projected to grow from USD 13.28 billion (2026) to USD 61.63 billion by 2034, at a CAGR of 21.2%. Government and municipal bodies are projected to grow at the fastest pace as they increasingly mandate zero-emission equipment in urban development and public works projects. North America currently holds a 32.8% market revenue share. Major OEMs including Volvo, Caterpillar, Komatsu, JCB, and Bobcat are shipping battery-electric models. Oslo has mandated zero-emission machinery for all municipal construction projects effective January 1, 2025 -- the first city-wide mandate of its kind.
