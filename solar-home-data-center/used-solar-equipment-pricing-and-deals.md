# Used Solar Equipment: Fair Pricing & Where the Best Deals Are

*A deep-dive buyer's guide for a DIY solar install — panels, inverters, batteries, and all the balance-of-system gear (wiring, connectors, racking, protection).*

**Compiled:** 2026-06-22 · **Market:** United States, 2025–2026
**Method:** 5 parallel research streams, cross-source verification, conflicting figures flagged.

> ⚠️ **Sourcing caveat (applies throughout):** Many dealer sites (SanTan Solar, Signature Solar, Current Connected, BatteryHookup, GreentecAuto, EnergySage, SolarReviews) block automated fetching (HTTP 403). Figures below come from search-indexed content, forum price-aggregation (DIY Solar Power Forum), and secondary coverage. Treat all prices as **representative ranges that fluctuate with inventory, copper spot price, and tariffs** — confirm live quotes before buying.

## TL;DR — The One-Page Verdict

| Component | Buy used? | Fair price (used/value) | Best deal source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Panels** | ✅ Yes (low risk) | **$0.10–$0.25/W** used; new B-grade $0.11–$0.30/W | Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist (local pickup); SanTan Solar; Signature Solar B-grade pallets |
| **Inverters** | ⚠️ Risky | ~50% of new (varies wildly); **dealer-refurb with warranty is safest** | DIY Solar Forum classifieds; Victron B-stock (InvertersRUs); ABS Alaskan refurb Sol-Ark |
| **Microinverters (Enphase)** | ⚠️ Account-lock risk | Get seller to "release" from Enlighten account first | eBay (verify release); SanTan Solar |
| **Batteries** | ⚠️ Often new DIY cells win | **~$100–$110/kWh new grade-A DIY cells** beats most "used"; usable used storage ~$110–$180/kWh | BatteryHookup, Greentec Auto, DIY Solar Forum, GobelPower/18650batterystore |
| **Wire / connectors / fuses / disconnects** | ❌ **Buy NEW** | See BoS table below | Current Connected, Signature Solar, WindyNation, electrical supply houses |
| **Racking** | ❌ Buy new (UL-2703 system) | ~$0.16/W (IronRidge); ~3% of system cost | Greentech Renewables, RES Supply (~30% off MSRP) |

**Three rules that fall out of the research:**
1. **Used panels = good. Used safety/electrical gear = bad.** Panels degrade gracefully and predictably; connectors, wire, and breakers are fire/code risk and barely cost anything new.
2. **"Used" batteries often lose to new DIY cells.** Once you derate a used pack for state-of-health and add a BMS/enclosure/labor, new grade-A LiFePO4 cells at ~$100/kWh frequently come out cheaper *and* warrantied.
3. **Don't overpay for "used" panels.** Above ~$0.30/W, new B-grade pallets (full output, often 1-yr warranty, no degradation) are the smarter buy.

## 1. Solar Panels

### Price per watt: used vs. new
- **Used panels: ~$0.10–$0.60/W** broadly, with one source widening the floor to **$0.05/W**. The huge spread reflects condition (like-new to heavily aged). [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/blog/all-about-used-solar-panels), [EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/solar/used-solar-panels-for-sale/)
- **Refurbished panels cost 50–70% less than new** (tested/restored, so priced above raw "used"). [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/blog/all-about-used-solar-panels)
- **New bare panels:** premium mono ≈ **$0.30–$0.50/W**, poly ≈ **$0.25/W**; a single new 400W ≈ $120–$200. [Solar.com](https://www.solar.com/learn/solar-panel-cost/), [EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/local-data/solar-panel-cost/)
- **New wholesale/pallet pricing has collapsed to $0.08–$0.28/W** (sub-$0.10 only for raw cells or container loads), which compresses the whole used market. [Accio](https://www.accio.com/plp/solar-panels-price-per-watt), [Preta Power](https://www.pretapower.com/photovoltaic-panels-price-guide-2025-residential-and-commercial-costs/)

> ⚠️ **Key conflict:** Because *new* B-grade pallets now run **$0.25–$0.30/W**, the used value proposition is thin at the high end — you'd pay *more* for an old used panel than a new B-grade one. pv-magazine confirms low new-module prices are actively depressing the secondhand market. [pv-magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2024/04/23/low-solar-module-prices-affecting-secondhand-market/)

### B-grade ≠ used (important distinction)
- **B-grade = new panels with cosmetic-only defects** (scratches, frame blemishes, no original packaging) that deliver **identical electrical output**; discount **20–35%**, landing **~$0.11–$0.30/W** in pallets. Forum buyers reported **~$65 for a 575W Signature Solar B-grade panel (≈$0.11/W)**. [A1 SolarStore](https://a1solarstore.com/clearance-solar-panels.html), [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/65-ea-575w-bifacial-panels-signature-solar-31-panel-pallet.125363/)
- **Truly used panels** carry real age degradation and hidden-defect risk (below).

### Where to buy
- **SanTan Solar (Gilbert, AZ)** — best-known US used/surplus dealer, ~26 yrs in business; ~$0.06/W on small low-wattage units up to ~$0.60/W after shipping; sells by unit and pallet; claims a **1-yr warranty** on blemished stock; **local pickup** avoids brutal freight on glass. [SanTan Solar](https://www.santansolar.com/condition/used/)
- **Signature Solar (TX)** — new **Grade-B pallets** (not truly used); e.g., Waaree 575W bifacial. [Signature Solar](https://signaturesolar.com/waaree-pallet-of-575w-bifacial-solar-panels-grade-b-31-panels/)
- **A1 SolarStore** — new 400W $150–$300; pallets as low as $0.26–$0.41/W; B-grade saves 20–35%. [A1 SolarStore](https://a1solarstore.com/clearance-solar-panels.html)
- **Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist / eBay** — the true "used" channel with a cult following; older panels often **<$50 each**; one buyer got a **385W JinkoSolar for $100 (~$0.26/W)** on Craigslist. [Fortune/Bloomberg, Apr 2025](https://fortune.com/article/used-solar-panels-sold-on-facebook-and-ebay-have-cult-following/)
- **SecondSol** — Europe's largest PV marketplace (~250k modules); demand for used currently low because new is so cheap. [SecondSol](https://www.secondsol.com/en/marketplace/)
- **Sun Electronics / Surplus Record** — surplus lots (~$75/panel), often full-pallet minimums. [Surplus Record](https://surplusrecord.com/electrical-power/solar-panels/)

### Risks of used panels
- **Degradation ~0.5–1%/yr** → a 10-yr-old panel outputs ~90–95% of nameplate, so effective $/W is higher than the sticker. [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/blog/all-about-used-solar-panels)
- **Hidden defects** (microcracks, corrosion, delamination, hot spots) are **not visible in photos** — experts advise EL imaging / professional testing. [Bob's Repair](https://solar.bobsrepair.com/the-dangers-of-used-solar-panels/), [Clean Energy Reviews](https://www.cleanenergyreviews.info/solar-panel-failure-degradation)
- **No warranty + installers won't touch them.** Used panels are effectively a **DIY-only** play; licensed installers refuse them because they can't extend workmanship warranties. [RxSun](https://rxsun.com/should-you-buy-used-solar-panels/)

### 💲 Fair price for a used 300–400W panel
- **Target $0.10–$0.25/W** in good, tested condition → roughly **$30–$100/panel**. The $100/385W Craigslist deal (~$0.26/W) is the realistic "good local find."
- **Don't pay over ~$0.30/W** — new B-grade pallets beat that.
- **Best deals:** local pickup (FB/Craigslist) for individuals; SanTan or Signature Solar B-grade pallets for volume.

## 2. Inverters & Charge Controllers

> 🔴 **General verdict: buying used inverters is the riskiest category.** Account locks, no warranty transfer, capacitor/fan aging, and 10–15 yr lifespans make them far riskier than panels. Prefer **dealer-refurbished units that retain warranty**, or new.

### Hybrid inverters (Sol-Ark, EG4)
- **New Sol-Ark 12K ~$4,500; 15K ~$6,000** (built-in MPPT, 10-yr warranty). [ShopSolarKits](https://shopsolarkits.com/products/sol-ark-15k)
- **Used Sol-Ark needs ≤~50% of new to sell** (≤~$2,250 for 12K, ≤~$3,000 for 15K); thin market, fast depreciation. [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/is-there-a-market-for-used-sol-ark-15k.83748/)
- **Dealer-refurb Sol-Ark 12K-2P with FULL manufacturer warranty at "less than half" of new** (ABS Alaskan) — the rare used path that keeps warranty. [ABS Alaskan](https://absak.com/product/used-solark-12k-all-in-one-mppt-solar-power-inverter/)
- **New EG4 18kPV ~$4,898–$5,000.** [Signature Solar](https://signaturesolar.com/eg4-18kpv-hybrid-inverter-eg4-18kpv-12lv-48v-split-phase-120-240vac-ul1741-cec/)
- **Used EG4 18kPV "great condition" listed $3,750** — only ~23–25% off new (used inverter discounts are often shallower than you'd hope). [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/3-750-used-great-condition-eg4-18kpv-for-sale-denver-co.124365/)

### Microinverters (Enphase)
- **Used IQ7/IQ7+ actively traded on eBay**, often pulled from decommissioned arrays. Exact per-unit price hard to pin (listing pages 403-blocked) — verify live. [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/305313435303)
- **SanTan Solar stocks IQ8+** as a surplus channel. [SanTan Solar](https://www.santansolar.com/product/enphase-iq8-microinverter-2/)

### MPPT charge controllers
- **New benchmarks:** EPEVER Tracer 4215BN <$200; Renogy Rover ~$320; MidNite Classic ~$600+. [SunForgeLab](https://sunforgelab.com/blog/reviews/best-mppt-charge-controllers-2026/)
- **Used Victron SmartSolar MPPT ~$175–$400** on forum classifieds (150/70 at $175; 250/100-Tr at $400). [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/sold-victron-mppt-250-100-tr-smartsolar-charge-controller-sf-bay-area-400.99831/)
- **Victron B-stock/open-box (InvertersRUs)** — fully tested, 1–4 yrs remaining warranty; **Victron's 5-yr warranty is transferable** (a major advantage over Enphase/SolarEdge). [InvertersRUs](https://invertersrus.com/victron-energy-b-stock-sale/)

### Risks (why used inverters bite)
- **Enphase cloud-account lock:** micros must be "released" from the seller's Enlighten account or you can't monitor/configure them. [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/if-i-purchase-used-enphase-microinverters-and-they-have-not-been-released.92727/)
- **SolarEdge installer-account lock:** changing optimizers needs installer admin access many won't grant. [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/how-to-change-solar-edge-optimizers-and-update-new-optimizer-serial-s-without-an-installer-id.109166/)
- **Wear vs. panels:** string inverters last only **~10–15 yr** (vs. 25–30 for panels); failure modes are **electrolytic-capacitor aging** (worse above ~50°C) and **fan wear**. [pv-magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2025/08/05/how-long-do-residential-solar-inverters-last-5/)
- **No warranty transfer (general)** + replace-don't-repair economics above ~40–50% of new cost. [Preta Power](https://www.pretapower.com/how-long-do-solar-inverters-last-lifespan-maintenance-and-replacement-guide/)

> ⚠️ **Conflicts:** Used discount depth swings by brand/channel (Sol-Ark "50% off" rule vs. only ~25% off a real EG4 listing vs. refurb-with-warranty "less than half"). Microinverters may last ~25 yr (near panel life), so their main risk is the *account lock*, not aging.

## 3. Batteries

> 🟡 **Surprising verdict: new DIY grade-A LiFePO4 cells (~$100/kWh) often beat "used."** Once you derate a used pack for state-of-health (SoH) and add a BMS, enclosure, and labor, "cheap" used storage frequently isn't.

### New LiFePO4 server-rack baseline (the price to beat)
- **EG4 LifePower4 V2 (5.12 kWh) ~$234/kWh**; WallMount 280Ah ~$230/kWh — cited as lowest-cost new rack brand. [SolarTech Online](https://solartechonline.com/), [Signature Solar](https://signaturesolar.com/)
- **SOK 48V 314Ah ~$187/kWh.** [DIY Solar Forum]
- **Budget promos dip to ~$120–$154/kWh** (EcoWorthy 48V 50Ah at $368 shipped = $154/kWh).

### DIY raw cells — the real low-cost benchmark
- **New grade-A EVE MB31 314Ah cells $74–$80/cell** → 16 cells ≈ 16 kWh for ~$1,100–$1,450 = **well under $100/kWh at cell level.** [GobelPower](https://gobelpower.com/), [18650 Battery Store](https://18650batterystore.com/)
- **Complete 48V/280Ah (~14.3 kWh) DIY build ~$1,500–$2,200 = ~$106/kWh all-in.** [SunForgeLab](https://sunforgelab.com/)
- **Grade-B/reclaimed prismatic cells save ~20–30%** → ~$60–$80/kWh at cell level (non-critical use).
- *Context (not a consumer price):* BloombergNEF puts 2025 LFP **pack wholesale at $70–$108/kWh.* [BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/)

### Second-life EV modules
- **Nissan Leaf Gen-1 modules ~$40 each (cases of 8) = ~$80/kWh nameplate** — but ~70% SoH, so effective ~$110–$115/kWh. [BatteryHookup](https://batteryhookup.com/)
- **Greentec Auto reman Leaf Gen-2 $120–$155/kWh retail; bulk $65–$70/kWh.** [Greentec Auto](https://greentecauto.com/)
- **Chevy Volt full pack (18.4 kWh) ~$3,000 = ~$152–$163/kWh.**
- **Used/reman Tesla packs:** 100 kWh $15,500 (~$155/kWh), 85 kWh $9,999 (~$118/kWh).
- **Big salvage bargains:** 102 kWh used Cadillac Lyriq pack ~$2,500 ≈ **$25/kWh nameplate** (raw, no BMS — large projects only). [DIY Solar Forum]
- **Academic range for integrated second-life: $44–$180/kWh**; retired packs leave vehicles at 70–80% capacity. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/), [IDTechEx](https://www.idtechex.com/)

### Used Tesla Powerwall
- **New PW3 $15,300–$16,200 before incentives.** Used market thin (~$7,000 each for year-old PW2, older anecdote). **Caveats:** account-transfer "a giant mess," can be **bricked** by deep discharge, needs **Tesla-certified commissioning.** [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/), [Tesla Motors Club](https://teslamotorsclub.com/)

### Used lead-acid / AGM — generally skip
- **$200–$350/kWh upfront**, barely cheaper used; lifetime cost is the trap: **~$2.55/kWh-cycle (AGM) vs. ~$0.38/kWh-cycle (LiFePO4).** [SolarTech Online](https://solartechonline.com/), [PowerTech Systems](https://www.powertechsystems.eu/)

### Reclaimed 18650 (DIY powerwall)
- **~$125/kWh effective**, but poor yield: ~40% test >2000mAh, ~40% <2000mAh, ~20% unusable — every cell needs capacity + internal-resistance testing. [Second Life Storage](https://secondlifestorage.com/)

### Risks
- **Three core hazards:** electrocution, hazardous cell contents, fire/thermal runaway (aged cells more prone). [MDPI Batteries 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/), [UK OPSS second-life BESS study](https://www.gov.uk/)
- **Unknown cycle history + uneven SoH** → must rebalance, add aftermarket BMS, derate. Second-life EV modules drift apart in the vehicle and need rebalancing.
- **No warranty / no UL listing** on raw modules → insurance and code problems.

### 💲 Realistic price for *usable* kWh
- **Cheapest defensible path: new grade-A DIY cells ~$100–$110/kWh all-in** (warrantied, full life).
- **Genuine used bargains $25–$115/kWh nameplate**, but after SoH derate + BMS/enclosure/labor, **realistic usable cost ~$110–$180/kWh.**
- **Best hunting grounds:** BatteryHookup (Leaf/module lots), Greentec Auto (bulk reman $65–$70/kWh), DIY Solar Forum classifieds + "Online Deals," GobelPower/18650batterystore (cells), eBay (Volt/Leaf/Powerwall), Facebook Tesla Energy groups.

> ⚠️ **Conflict:** "Second-life $/kWh" ranges from $0–$60 (raw retired modules) to $44–$180 (integrated) to $25 (one-off salvage) to $80–$155 (retail reman). The spread is mostly **nameplate-raw vs. usable-integrated** and SoH. Don't conflate cell/pack price with installed-system price ($450–$1,000/kWh).

## 4. Balance of System (Wiring, Connectors, Racking, Protection)

> 🔴 **Verdict: buy this category NEW.** It's cheap relative to the system, and counterfeit/used connectors and wire are a documented fire and code-compliance risk. DC connectors are implicated in **~24–27% of solar-related fires** (European studies). [SolarVision AI](https://solarvisionai.com/mc4-connectors-solar-safety-installation-guide/)

### Wire & cable
| Item | Typical price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 10 AWG PV wire | **~$0.55/ft** | [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/best-prices-for-pv-wire.42169/) |
| 8 AWG PV wire | **~$0.95/ft** | DIY Solar Forum |
| 8 AWG battery cable | **~$0.90/ft** | DIY Solar Forum |
| 2 AWG welding/battery cable | **~$3.25/ft** | [SolarPanelStore](https://www.solarpanelstore.com/products/cobra-x-flex-2-0-awg-cable-red) |
| 4/0 Ancor marine battery cable | **~$4.33/ft** | [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/4-0-ancor-battery-cable-4-33-ft.110912/) |
| 2/0 cable | **~$3–$7/ft** (US-made vs. marine vs. import) | [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/best-online-cable-00.85558/) |

*US-made flexible copper (TEMCo, WindyNation EXCELENE) is the value pick; prices float with copper spot price.*

### Connectors
- **Quality MC4 pairs ~$6/pair**; cheap non-certified clones **$0.25–$0.50/pair** (do not use). [Solar-Stack](https://www.solar-stack.com/en/blog/mc4-solar-connectors-guide)
- **Branch/Y connectors** (2-to-1, 3-to-1) at AIMS, Grape Solar (Home Depot), Signature Solar, Powerwerx. [Home Depot Grape Solar](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Grape-Solar-MC4-Comparable-Branch-Connector-Set-Male-Female-GS-BCS-MC4/206545630)
- **NEC 2020 690.33(C):** connectors must be listed as same-type/same-manufacturer or listed intermateable — inspectors **fail mixed-brand MC4**. Verify UL numbers (counterfeit "UL" connectors are common on Amazon/Temu/eBay). [Conversions Tech](https://conversionstech.com/blogs/news/mc4-solar-connector-compatibility-nec-ul-guide-2026)
- **Counterfeit-Stäubli tell:** genuine Stäubli does **not** use red O-rings (red = fake). [SolarGrade](https://solargrade.io/articles/connector-findings-from-hundreds-of-inspections/)

### Racking / mounting
- **IronRidge residential ~$0.16/W** + roof attachments (~$200 for S-5 feet); typically ~30% under MSRP via distributors. [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/ironridge-mounting-hardware-prices.51376/)
- **Racking ≈ ~3% of total system cost** (~$1,068 for an 11 kW system). Unirac cheaper upfront; IronRidge more rugged. [Nuance Energy](https://nuanceenergy.com/solar-blog/ironridge-vs-unirac-mounted-solar-options-cost-benefits)
- **Ground mount ~$0.30–$0.50/W more than roof** (footings/concrete/trenching); EnergySage puts ground ~51% more expensive overall. [EnergySage](https://www.energysage.com/solar/alternatives-to-rooftop-solar/ground-mounted-solar/)
- **Must be UL-2703-listed as an integrated system** (rails + clamps + bonding) for NEC 690.43/250 — used/mismatched racking risks failing the listing. [ExpertCE](https://expertce.com/learn-articles/how-to-select-solar-racking-system/)

### Combiner boxes, disconnects, fuses, busbars
- **MidNite MNPV6 combiner** (6-string, ETL-listed) at Current Connected, EcoDirect, RES Supply. [Current Connected](https://www.currentconnected.com/product/midnite-solar-mnpv3-mnpv6-mnpv12-combiner-breaker-box/)
- **Victron 6-way MEGA fuse holder + 250A busbar $44.19.** [Off Grid Stores](https://offgridstores.com/products/victron-6-way-mega-fuse-holder-w-busbar-250a)
- **IMO rooftop DC isolators** (Signature Solar); **Square D HU362RB 60A 600V disconnect** (SolarPanelStore). [SolarPanelStore](https://www.solarpanelstore.com/products/square-d-hu362rb-60a-600v-3p-non-fusible-nema3r)
- DIN-rail PV fuses, Class T fuses, busbars at Current Connected, SanTan, TheSolarStore.

### BoS as % of total cost
- **Rule of thumb: BoS adds ~$0.30–$0.50/W** (racking, wiring, disconnects, monitoring). [Anern](https://www.anernstore.com/blogs/costs-incentives-policy/solar-installation-cost-breakdown-2025)
- ⚠️ **Conflict:** Some sources say BoS = 30–60% of total, but that broad definition folds in inverters/labor/soft costs. **Hardware-only BoS** (the wiring/racking/protection asked about here) is closer to **~10–20% of a residential system.** [Greentech Renewables](https://www.greentechrenewables.com/article/balance-solar-pv-systems-bos), [NREL Q1-2024 Benchmarks](https://data.nrel.gov/submissions/307)

## 5. Where the Best Deals Are + How to Buy Safely

### Best venues for deals
- **SanTan Solar** — new/used/refurb/overstock panels + BoS; strong on bulk and **local pickup** (avoids freight). [SanTan Solar](https://www.santansolar.com/)
- **Signature Solar** — broad BoS catalog; **"End-of-Summer Savings" (up to 50% off) is the cited best buying window**; 0% financing over $200. [Signature Solar](https://signaturesolar.com/)
- **Current Connected** — Victron, MidNite, fuses/breakers. **GoGreenSolar, Unbound Solar, WindyNation, EcoDirect, Wire & Cable Your Way** — cable & racking. **Home Depot/Lowes** — THHN by-the-foot, Grape Solar connectors (convenient, not cheapest). **Electrical supply houses** (Greentech Renewables, RES Supply) — IronRidge/Unirac ~30% off MSRP.
- **Liquidation / auctions** — real channel for commercial-grade gear: **HGP Auction** (bankruptcy lots — modules, storage, racking, inverters), **GovDeals** (renewable-energy category), **GovPlanet** (weekly govt surplus), **Liquidation.com** (retailer closeouts). [HGP](https://www.hgpauction.com/auctions/123968/sunworks-bk/), [GovDeals](https://www.govdeals.com/en/renewable-energy-generation)
- **EnergyBin** — DOE-funded, members-only **B2B** marketplace for surplus/decommissioned/refurbished solar, often **warrantied** from vetted sellers (the reputable used-commercial channel). [EnergyBin](https://energybin.com/)
- **Decommissioned/repowered solar farms** — a growing source of cheap used panels as owners replace still-good panels with higher-efficiency models. [GreenLancer](https://www.greenlancer.com/post/decommissioning-solar-panels)

### Timing & market context (2025–2026)
- **Module spot prices ROSE to ~$0.28/W in Q1 2026** (up from ~$0.25/W early 2025) — AD/CVD tariffs + FEOC compliance pushing prices **up**, not down. [pv-magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/04/03/u-s-solar-module-prices-face-upward-pressure-as-trade-risks-and-feoc-rules-dominate-q1-2026/)
- **The 30% federal Section 25D residential credit ended Dec 31, 2025** (One Big Beautiful Bill Act). The post-credit 2026 demand drop may soften installed-system/retail prices even as module tariffs rise. [Solar.com](https://www.solar.com/learn/solar-pricing-in-2026/)
- **California NEM 3.0 (upheld in court, March 2026)** cut export pay ~75%; battery attachment jumped from 11% to >50%. Effect: **CA solar-only resale value is depressed** → used panels from upgraded CA systems are a buyer's opportunity. [pv-magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/03/10/california-court-upholds-nem-3-0-dealing-blow-to-rooftop-solar/)

### How to vet used gear
- **Panels:** measure **Voc, Isc, and power under load** with a CAT III 600V/1000V multimeter + DC clamp meter; flag anything below ~90% of spec (temp/irradiance-adjusted). [PVEducation](https://www.pveducation.org/pvcdrom/modules-and-arrays/module-measurement-with-load)
- **Gold standard: IV-curve tracing** (Solmetric PVA-1500, HT IV400) reveals cell mismatch/degradation that spot Voc/Isc checks miss. [Fluke](https://www.fluke.com/en-us/learn/blog/renewable-energy/comparing-i-v-curve-tracing-to-circuit-testing-solar-modules)
- **Inverters:** verify warranty transferability/registration. SolarEdge transfers free *if equipment stays in place* (inverter warranty only ~12 yr). Enphase transfer needs registration + internet within 45 days; a detached used micro is a warranty risk. [Enphase Transfer of Ownership](https://enphase.com/store/services/transfer-ownership), [SolarEdge warranty](https://www.solaredge.com/us/warranty)

### Scams to avoid
- **Counterfeit MC4 connectors** — look identical, ~1/10 price, documented arc-fire hazard (red O-rings = fake). [efixx](https://www.facebook.com/100063595862417/posts/1250090770454107/)
- **Underpriced Facebook panels** are frequently **stolen or counterfeit** — red flags: prices far below market, upfront third-party-app payment to "hold," stolen photos, no packaging/receipts, vague provenance. [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/solar-panel-scam.92888/page-2), [Solar.com](https://www.solar.com/learn/solar-panel-scams/)

### Permitting / insurance / warranty downsides
- **AHJs require UL-listed, NEC-compliant equipment** (modules, inverters, racking, rapid-shutdown) with spec sheets — old/non-listed gear can fail permitting/inspection. [PTO Edge](https://ptoedge.com/blog/documents-required-for-solar-permits-in-the-usa/)
- **Unpermitted solar:** fines $500–$10,000+, can **void homeowners insurance**, complicate home sale/title, force retroactive permitting or removal. Some states (e.g., TX) legally require a licensed electrician for interconnection. [SolarTech Online](https://solartechonline.com/blog/solar-panel-permit-requirements-guide/)
- **Manufacturer warranties commonly require certified-pro install**; DIY/improper install voids them, and used panels without transferable paperwork have no coverage. [Summit Solar & Battery](https://summitsolarandbattery.com/blog/understanding-solar-equipment-warranties/)

### Mixing used components
- **Electrically safe but lossy.** Match voltage for parallel strings, current for series strings; verify Voc/Isc/wattage. **Best handled with microinverters or optimizers** so mismatched used panels don't drag a whole string. [ShopSolar](https://shopsolarkits.com/blogs/learning-center/mixing-different-wattage-solar-panels)

## Confidence & Caveats
- **High confidence:** used panels are low-risk and a good buy under ~$0.30/W; BoS/safety gear should be new; new DIY LiFePO4 cells (~$100/kWh) are the value benchmark for storage; inverters are the riskiest used category (account locks, short life).
- **Flagged conflicts:** used-panel $/W floor ($0.05 vs. $0.10); BoS share of cost (10–20% hardware-only vs. 30–60% broad); second-life battery $/kWh ($25 raw salvage vs. $44–$180 integrated); used-inverter discount depth (25% vs. 50%).
- **Sourcing limitation:** many dealer pages returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching, so several prices come from search snippets and forum aggregation rather than direct page reads. **Confirm live quotes before purchasing** — solar pricing moves with copper, tariffs, and inventory.
